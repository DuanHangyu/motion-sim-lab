THE SCIENCE OF FALLING INTO STEP

A rhythm you can touch.

Each swing follows the nonlinear equation θ″ = −(g/ℓeff) sin θ − cθ′, with g = 9.81 m/s² and c = 0.004 s⁻¹. Bigger releases have slightly longer periods.

The center-to-pivot length includes the finite bob radius: ℓeff = L + 0.4r²/L. Rods and hubs are light; the rigid frame has no coupling or collisions.

The model integrates at 480 steps per second and stores 120 samples per second. Every bob, phase hand, ghost, curve, and event uses the same simulation time. The right side of a curve is a prediction from that same model.

Push either bob independently. Scrub backwards to before the push and replay it. A new push in the past replaces later pushes. Length or release-angle changes start both swings together again.

Drag a bob down/up to change length, sideways to set a common release angle; click or flick it to push. Drag a dial to tune, click to find a simple ratio. Drag the glass timeline or click a marker. Background drag orbits; right drag pans; scroll zooms.

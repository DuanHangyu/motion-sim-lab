In one breath

Fourteen labs, one island. Each lab is a creature, and its size is its score on the lens you have picked: the biggest monster on the island is today's leader, and it wears the crown. Change the lens at the top and the whole roster walks to its new place.

What is real and what is not

The ranks, the numbers and the model names are real, taken from public benchmarks the way airace.lol reads them, with a dated copy inside the page as a fallback. The creatures, colours and abilities are art: a lab keeps the same form on every lens, so you can follow it around.

A lens only lists the models it has actually tested. Where a lab's newest model has not been run, or its only result is more than a year old, the monster shows no score on that lens, shrinks to seed size and sits at the back. No number is ever invented. Context is the largest window among a lab's models released in the last year.

Play with it

Click any monster (or its row) to open its card: every lens at once, with a ✦ on each top-three place. Tap a row in the card to switch the island to that lens. C hands the camera to the director; your first drag takes it back.

Keys

1–7 pick a lens · C cinematic cameras · R reset the view · ⇧N show or hide every name (hover a monster for its own) · ⇧T tilt-shift · / hide the interface · Esc close the card · ⌘' render tuning · drag to orbit · right-drag to pan · scroll to zoom · WASD to move, Q E to turn, hold ⇧ for faster.

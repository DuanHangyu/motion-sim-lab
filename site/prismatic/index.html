THE OPTICS BENCH

Follow the light. Place an element, adjust its properties, and see how every intersection changes the path.

PLACE
Choose a tool, then click or tap the table. Up to 24 elements.
MOVE
Drag any element. Use the inspector for exact positions.
ROTATE
Shift + drag, use [ and ], or adjust Rotation in the inspector.
CAMERA
Hold ⌘ (Command) or Ctrl and drag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. On touch screens, turn on ORBIT, then drag. Use + / − to zoom and ↺ to restore the view.
VISUALS
FX adds soft reflections, contact shading and a restrained glow. Turn it off for a lighter rendering mode.
PROBE
Place a probe on a ray to measure wavelength, direction, intensity and optical distance.
KEYBOARD
Hold Shift: ⇧V select · ⇧L lens · ⇧P prism · ⇧M mirror · ⇧B splitter · ⇧A iris · ⇧T target · ⇧Q probe. Arrows move, Delete removes, ⇧S toggles snapping.
TIME
Trace Rays replays propagation. The time slider slows light for observation; it is not physical travel time.

Snell’s law governs refraction and total internal reflection. Lenses use a thin-lens approximation. White light is sampled into seven colors at the first dispersive surface.

X Muse

A round plaza at blue hour where the timeline is something you can stand under. Every glowing ribbon is a feed, every card on it a post, and the fluffy creatures are the people: they ride the posts, lean over the balconies to read them, and pass hearts to the ones they like.

Post something

Write in What's happening? and press Post. Your muse throws it onto the ribbon beside you. As it loops past the crowd, creatures who like it send hearts across the plaza, a few repost it onto the big spiral, and the screens pick it up. The bell counts what comes back.

Read and react

Click any card to pull it out of the ribbon. ♥ sends a heart back to whoever wrote it, wherever they are standing. Repost copies it onto another ribbon, reply sends your muse's bubble, stats shows who saw it.

Why X?

Because this week X is flooded with Muse, so the plaza opened as X. To be fair, the mark at the top left switches every logo in it: Everyone (the default, a mix that turns the monument every few seconds), X, Threads, Instagram or Meta. On Threads and Meta about half the crowd turns up.

Three views, three modes

For you is the plaza at eye level. Following rides along behind a surfer on the ribbon. Explore looks straight down on the rings. Orbit lets you fly anywhere; Muse puts you in your creature (walk with W A S D, Space to jump, and jump onto a passing card to surf the timeline); Feed lists every post on the ribbons and flies you to it. Click any creature to make it your muse.

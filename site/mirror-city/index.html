How The Reflection works

Every tower is one AI model on OpenRouter. Above the floor, its height is the tokens it processed in the last seven days. Below the floor, its amber reflection is as deep as the typical cost of one session on it: the median of real sessions in the agent you pick, at the length you pick.

A real reflection is as tall as the thing it reflects. These are not. The towers doing most of the work throw almost no reflection, and a few low buildings throw reflections deeper than any skyscraper is tall. That is the point: the models people use most are not where the money goes.

What you can do

Session length (1 turn to 50+ turns): every reflection stretches as sessions get longer, because each turn sends the whole conversation again. Drag up and down on the glass front of the vitrine to scrub it. Agent: the same models cost different amounts in Hermes Agent, Claude Code, Codex and Kilo Code. Click a tower for its numbers.

What is exact and what is not

Heights and depths are both linear: twice the tokens is twice as tall, twice the cost is twice as deep, on one scale for every agent and length (the ruler on the glass). The smallest towers are drawn at least one storey tall. Towers with no reflection had no sessions measured: stealth and free models, or models listed after the cost window closed (they are still under construction). A tower glows cyan inside when it carries at least 1% of the week's tokens and one session on it costs under the threshold in the card's sentence; the rest glow warm. Costs are medians, not averages, and tokens are counted by each provider's own tokenizer, so a token on one model is not exactly a token on another.

    In one breath

    Seven rooms, from the 2017 paper that started it all to the models released this month. Each room is an era, each artifact is a real model, and the artifacts get more complex as you walk: stone tablets for the first language models, bronze for the scale era, marble for the chat era, glass and crystal for the models that see, listen and reason. The last room is the Grand Hall, where every lab's best model today is on display.

    What is real and what is not

    Names, dates and the facts on every card are real (release dates, sizes, context windows, what each model changed). Today's numbers are live: the Intelligence Index from Artificial Analysis, read through airace.lol, with a dated copy inside the page as a fallback. Scores on that index exist for models from 2025 on; older models are described, not scored. The artifacts, statues and slogans are art.

    Play with it

    Press ▶ for the guided tour, or pick a year on the timeline. Click any artifact for its card, and use ← → to walk the timeline one model at a time. Hover a Hall of Fame row to light that lab's line through the museum, or press / to search any model, lab or era.

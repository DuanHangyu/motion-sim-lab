How to use

Click any robot on the floor or in the list to inspect it. Drag to orbit, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom.
  • Block aisle arms a tool: click the floor to drop a barricade. With Auto reroute off, traffic piles up until you press Reroute fleet.
  • Rush order injects a priority job with a 30 s SLA. Charger offline takes one pad down. Fault stalls the selected robot.
  • Fleet + / − adds or removes robots (4–60). Watch queue time and delayed orders as the floor gets crowded.

One mountain for every skill

In one breath

Each of the five peaks is a skill: coding, reasoning, math, agents, creativity. Each lab is a climber, and how high it stands is how well its best model has done on that peak's benchmarks. On the Overview every lab stands on the peak where it beats the field by the most. Pick a peak to send everyone there, or click a climber to see where it stands on all five.

The timeline

The slider replays real history: each lab's best result by that date, from the end of 2023 to today. Labs with nothing tested yet wait at base camp. Nobody falls back down: a result is the best any of the lab's models had reached by then.

What is real and what is not

Heights, ranks, models and dates are real benchmark results. The altitude score is the mean height of the benchmarks a lab has been tested on, each read on its own scale, so a hard test that nobody has cracked keeps the summit out of reach. Overall is the mean over the peaks a lab has been tested on (at least two). Nothing is ever invented: a peak a lab has no result on shows —. The climbers, the mountains and the weather are art.
